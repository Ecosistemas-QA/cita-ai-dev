### EPIC 1: Gestión de Cuentas de Profesional (Admin)
**Planned Jira Key:** CITAAI-5
**Priority:** CRITICAL
**Description:** Permite a los profesionales crear y gestionar su identidad en la plataforma.

**User Stories (estimado: 4):**
1.  **CITAAI-29** - As a professional (Laura), I want to be able to register with my name, email and password to create my account in Cita.ai.
2.  **CITAAI-30** - As a professional (Carlos), I want to be able to log in with my email and password to access my administration panel.

4.  **CITAAI-9** - As a professional (Carlos), I want a unique and simple public URL to be generated for my profile (e.g. `cita.ai/carlos-rojas`) so that I can easily share it on my Instagram or WhatsApp.
